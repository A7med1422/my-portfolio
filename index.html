About Me

I care about the part most demos skip: what happens when the input is messy, the network is gone, and the person using it has no interest in how the model works.

My work sits between research and product. I choose the evaluation before the architecture, keep inference fast enough to feel immediate, and design output a person can question. That's as true for a forecasting dashboard as it is for a real-time sign language translator.
